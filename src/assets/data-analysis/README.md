# Bundled analysis examples

These are real, unmodified CSVs. They are bundled so examples work offline. Original missing-value tokens are preserved.

## Palmer Penguins

- File: `penguins.csv`, 344 rows and 8 columns.
- Source: https://github.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins/blob/8957207b78d6ccd1b4654a9dd9c9041b657478ab/inst/extdata/penguins.csv
- License: CC0 1.0, https://github.com/allisonhorst/palmerpenguins/blob/8957207b78d6ccd1b4654a9dd9c9041b657478ab/LICENSE.md
- Credit: Gorman KB, Williams TD, Fraser WR (2014), *Ecological Sexual Dimorphism and Environmental Variability within a Community of Antarctic Penguins*. PLoS ONE 9(3): e90081. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0090081
- Package: Horst AM, Hill AP, Gorman KB (2020), *palmerpenguins: Palmer Archipelago (Antarctica) penguin data*. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3960218
- Units: bill/flipper measurements in mm, body mass in g. `NA` denotes missing observations.

## Seattle Weather

- File: `seattle-weather.csv`, 1,461 rows and 6 columns, daily data from 2012 through 2015.
- Source: https://github.com/vega/vega-datasets/blob/5448e25297c8f705be78e879d5423d55d04dc6f1/data/seattle-weather.csv
- Original source: NOAA National Climatic Data Center.
- Data license: U.S. government public-domain data, per Vega's `datapackage.json` (the repository's BSD license is for its code).
- Temperature in °C, precipitation in mm, wind in m/s. The `weather` category is synthesized by Vega from original fields for instructional use.
- Metadata: https://github.com/vega/vega-datasets/blob/5448e25297c8f705be78e879d5423d55d04dc6f1/datapackage.json
