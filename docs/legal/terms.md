# Archimedes Terms of Service

Status: draft for the collaboration beta.

Archimedes is research software. You are responsible for the content you upload,
for having the right to share it, and for complying with your institution's
research-data policies. Do not use the cloud service for data you are not
permitted to process off-device.

Cloud features are provided as-is during beta. Local features remain available
without an account. We may change limits, providers, or availability with
notice. Do not abuse shared links, agent runs, or storage quotas.

This draft must be reviewed by counsel before a paid public launch.
